Free restaurant billing with KOT and running tables

Take the order at the table, send a KOT to the kitchen, keep several tables running, and settle each bill with the right GST in a tap. It works on a phone, tablet or counter computer, offline too, with no login and no monthly fee.

Loading the restaurant bill maker…

How to make a restaurant bill here

  1. Set up once. Open "Restaurant details and bill settings": name, address, GSTIN and UPI ID, and the GST that applies to you: 5% for most restaurants, 18% inside a hotel with room tariffs above ₹7,500 a day, composition or not registered.
  2. Open a table. Choose Dine-in and type the table number, or Takeaway / Delivery. Add dishes: saved dishes come up with their price.
  3. Send the KOT. Press "Print KOT (new items)". Only dishes not yet sent go to the kitchen, with your notes ("less spicy"). Add more dishes later and print another KOT.
  4. Keep tables running. Press "Save table, take next order". Running tables show as buttons at the top; tap one to go back to it.
  5. Settle the bill. Add a discount or, if the guest agrees, a service charge. Split the bill among guests if they ask. Pick cash, UPI, card or credit and press "Settle and print bill".
  6. See the day. Every settled bill is saved under Saved bills and counts as a sale in the free Nivesh Sahayak App.

What it does for you

  • Running tables: any number of open orders at once, each with its own KOTs, saved in the browser so a reload never loses them.
  • Kitchen order tickets: only new items, with quantity and kitchen notes, numbered KOT 1, 2, 3 for each table.
  • One GST rate per bill: 5% (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%) or 18%, worked out on food, less discount, plus any service charge.
  • Split bill: shows what each person pays.
  • Thermal or A4: 80 mm and 58 mm rolls for the counter, A5 or A4 for a company guest who wants a full bill with their GSTIN.
  • UPI QR: an unsettled bill prints a UPI QR code with the exact amount.

Your data stays with you

Everything you type is kept in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Your businesses, customers, suppliers and items are shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak document tools through my.niveshsahayak.in, so details you saved while making an invoice or a quotation are already here as suggestions.

Keep a backup. If the browser's data is cleared, saved work in it is gone. Open My data (top of the page) and press Save a backup file now and then. One file holds every document, customer and item, and restores them on any other computer or phone.

Questions people ask

Is this restaurant billing free?

Yes. Billing, KOTs, running tables, printing and sharing are free, with no login, no limit and no monthly fee. A small line at the bottom of the bill says where it was made.

Which GST rate should my restaurant charge?

Most restaurants charge 5% without input tax credit. Restaurants inside hotels with room tariffs above ₹7,500 a day charge 18% with credit. See GST on restaurant bills.

Can I print KOTs?

Yes. "Print KOT (new items)" prints only the dishes not yet sent to the kitchen, numbered per table. See KOT.

Can I keep several tables open?

Yes. Save a table and open the next; running tables show as buttons at the top with their amount so far.

Can I split a bill?

Yes. Type the number of people under "Split the bill among"; the bill shows each person’s share.

Can I add a service charge?

Only with the guest’s agreement, under the CCPA guidelines of 2022. It is off by default. See service charge rules.

Does it work on my thermal printer?

Yes, 80 mm and 58 mm rolls, plus A5 and A4. In the print window set Margins to None and untick Headers and footers.

Where are my bills kept?

In this browser on this device, shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak tools through my.niveshsahayak.in. Nothing is sent to us. Save a backup file regularly.
